## Hook — open with one of these, never a throat-clear
- A NUMBER or stat: "100 nouveaux livres sont publiés chaque jour…", "1 100 navires aveugles".
- A QUESTION / puzzle: "Combien vaut x ?", "Quelle est la surface du carré ?", "La Chine a-t-elle fait un nouveau grand bond ?".
- A DATED SCENE: "17 janvier 2007. le vol AF084 Paris–San Francisco atteint son altitude de croisière…".
The first line is the whole battle: it must create curiosity or a stake in <12 words.

## Coin a memorable label (one per post)
Invent or curate a sticky handle that becomes the share-phrase:
"Cloud LIDL", "navires aveugles", "l'Empire AU Milieu", "enshittification". Name the thing.

## Format
- Punchy first line, then whitespace. Short paragraphs.
- Lists of three with arrow/emoji bullets (→ • 💶 📚). One explicit STAKE (souveraineté, éthique, a lesson).
- Plain text out-reaches link/article posts: put the take in plain text, link in a comment.

## Choose the mode on purpose
- REACH mode (puzzles, counter-intuitive facts) → maximizes impressions, lower reaction rate. Top-of-funnel visibility.
- DEPTH mode (date-anchored story, personal milestone + vulnerability, tribute to a named person) → drives COMMENTS and followers. Relationship.
Comments (highest-value engagement) come from: puzzles, nostalgia/personal, and opinion/debate pieces.

## Topic lane (your differentiated territory)
Tech × society × sovereignty × ethics, with INSIDER proof points (named institutions/media:
BFM, Les Echos, WIPO, Vatican, Assemblée nationale). Recurring winners: sovereign infra,
AI governance/ethics, geopolitics of tech, security/privacy, French/European tech.

## Voice cues (echo 07_Style_Profile)
Erudite-but-warm French; rhetorical figures (anaphora "le mérite lui revient…", aphorism
"si c'est gratuit, vous êtes le produit"); first-person vulnerability for milestones; credit
named people generously; throughline to the book "De mémoire vive".

## Cadence / format-for-goal
- Best slots: late morning Paris (≈10–11h, 13h); Saturday morning is a sleeper viral slot.
- Keep video SHORT (avg watch ≈ 26–35s); don't over-invest in long video.
- Posting itself drives audience: post-days show ~3× impressions and +57% new followers vs quiet days.

## Anti-patterns (avoid)
- Throat-clearing or vague openers; link-only posts (algorithm-suppressed); buzzwords
  ("révolutionnaire", "disruptif", "synergies"); more than ~one emoji per idea.
