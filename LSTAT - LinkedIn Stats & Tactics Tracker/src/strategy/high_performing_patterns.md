# High-Performing LinkedIn Patterns — Philippe Dewost
Data-backed from the best-posts analysis (67 exported posts × per-post metrics,
365-day account series). **PERFORMANCE STRATEGY ONLY** — what fares well.

> VOICE IS THE FLOOR. Tone, rhythm, retournement, punchline, lexical territory and
> forbidden forms are owned by 07_Style_Profile (system_prompt_short.md). These
> patterns are applied STRICTLY WITHIN that voice and must never override it.

## Hook — first line must create curiosity or a stake (never a throat-clear)
- A number/stat EMBEDDED IN A CLAIM — never a bare number as the title (the voice
  guide forbids "chiffre seul en titre"): "100 nouveaux livres sont publiés chaque jour…".
- A QUESTION / puzzle: "Combien vaut x ?", "La Chine a-t-elle fait un nouveau grand bond ?".
- A DATED SCENE: "17 janvier 2007. le vol AF084 atteint son altitude de croisière…".
First line = the whole battle: curiosity or a stake in under ~12 words.

## Coin a memorable label (one per post)
Invent/curate a sticky handle that becomes the share-phrase: "Cloud LIDL",
"navires aveugles", "l'Empire AU Milieu", "enshittification". Name the thing.

## Format
- Punchy first line, then whitespace. Short paragraphs.
- Lists of three with arrow/emoji bullets (→ • 💶). One explicit STAKE.
- Plain text out-reaches link/article posts: put the take in plain text, link in a comment.

## Choose the mode on purpose
- REACH mode (puzzles, counter-intuitive facts) → max impressions, lower reaction rate.
- DEPTH mode (date-anchored story, personal milestone, tribute to a named person) →
  drives COMMENTS + followers. Comments (highest-value engagement) come from puzzles,
  nostalgia/personal, and opinion/debate pieces.

## Topic lane (your differentiated territory)
Tech × society × sovereignty × ethics, with INSIDER proof points (named institutions/
media: BFM, Les Echos, WIPO, Vatican, Assemblée nationale). Winners: sovereign infra,
AI governance/ethics, geopolitics of tech, security/privacy, French/European tech.

## Cadence / format-for-goal
- Best slots: late morning Paris (≈10–11h, 13h); Saturday morning is a sleeper viral slot.
- Keep video SHORT (avg watch ≈ 26–35s); don't over-invest in long video.
- Posting drives audience: post-days ≈3× impressions and +57% new followers vs quiet days.

## Performance anti-patterns (STRUCTURAL only — voice/lexical anti-patterns live in the voice guide)
- Link-only / article-link posts (algorithm-suppressed) → put the take in plain text.
- More than ~one emoji per idea.
